# Bangladesh pricing hypotheses (BDT/month)

These are positioning experiments, not verified market prices or profit forecasts.

| Tier | Price | Offer |
|---|---:|---|
| Menu | 499 | QR menu, item management, one branch |
| Smart | 1,499 | QR ordering, KDS, staff dashboard, cash recording, reports |
| AI Pro | 2,999 | Smart plus bounded AI conversations and advanced insights |
| Enterprise | Quote | Multi-branch, integrations, priority support, custom limits |

Optional onboarding: BDT 2,000-10,000 depending on menu digitization and training. Offer time-limited pilots, not perpetual free custom implementations.

AI Pro needs per-tenant fair-use limits and usage metering. Payment gateway charges, SMS, extra branches, large AI volumes and hardware should be separately disclosed.

Unit economics: contribution margin = subscription - hosting - database - AI inference - payment/platform fees - support - sales commissions. Measure real pilot usage before committing to price or SLA.
